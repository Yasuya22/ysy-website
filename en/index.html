FAQ

Ask us anything.

What can I ask you to do?

Websites, design, social media, documents, video editing, events, marketing and all kinds of business support. If it's not listed, just ask.

Can I just ask for advice?

Yes. Even if nothing is decided yet, feel free to reach out. We'll listen and help you sort out what you need.

How is the price decided?

We quote based on the work, volume and deadline. You'll know the price up front, and we start only after you agree.

Can we meet in person?

Yes. Besides online and phone, we meet in person at our Nakano office, Machida and anywhere in Tokyo and Kanagawa.

Can you handle rush jobs?

Often, depending on our schedule and the job. Let us know your deadline when you contact us. Rush service may cost extra.

Do you offer revisions and support after delivery?

Yes. We handle revisions and updates after delivery, plus ongoing support such as social media, website management and other tasks.
