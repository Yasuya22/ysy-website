A message from the founder

In 2026, I started YSY General Services because I wanted to take on something new.

I have run a retail business, worked at an international research institute in Bangkok, managed and owned restaurants in Nihonbashi Ningyocho, and organised events behind the scenes. Having worked across different industries and countries, I understand the stage where you are not even sure what to ask for, and I am happy to start from there with you.

I enjoy focused, hands-on work on my own, and I am just as comfortable leading a large team. From a single web page to an entire event, I will fly in like a hero to solve your problem.
